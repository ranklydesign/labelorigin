Natural Baby Specification FAQ

What is left out of your baby formulations?

Our baby formulations are free of parabens, phthalates, synthetic dyes, artificial fragrances, petrochemicals, and harsh sulfates, and are made with gentle botanicals. As with any skincare product, we suggest a small patch test, and checking with a pediatrician for newborns.

What is the MOQ for private label baby care products?

Our stock wholesale baby products start at just 12+ units per SKU. Custom formulation R&D for pediatric lines begins at 250+ units.

Can I order sample kits of the baby line before ordering inventory?

Yes. Sample evaluation jars and bottles are provided at $3 to $5 each so pediatricians, parents, and brand founders can verify gentleness and texture.

What packaging options are best for baby care?

We offer single-hand dispensing pumps, flip-top tubes, foamer bottles for tearless wash, and recyclable jars with safety inner seals.
