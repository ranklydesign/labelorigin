Formulation Science • Theoretical Foundation

Cuticle Scale Mechanics & The Isoelectric Point of Hair Keratin

Hair fibers consist of an outer cuticle layer of overlapping imbricate scales protecting the inner cortex. When exposed to alkaline tap water and harsh high-pH sulfate cleansers (pH 7.0–8.5), the cuticle scales swell open and develop a strong negative electrostatic charge, leading to lipid loss, color fading, tangling, and mechanical breakage. Renaud Naturals formulates all botanical shampoos and conditioners between pH 4.5 and 5.0—the optimal range that flattens cuticle scales, locks in moisture, and restores high-gloss specular light reflection.

SUBTOPIC 01 • SURFACTANT MICELLAR DYNAMICS CHAPTER 01

Sulfate-Free Cleansing & Anionic-Amphoteric Synergy

Cheap shampoos rely on sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS), which strips the critical 18-methyleicosanoic acid (18-MEA) lipid layer from the hair shaft. Renaud formulates with a synergistic matrix of Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate (a gentle ester derived from coconut fatty acids) and Cocamidopropyl Betaine. This creates dense, creamy lamellar micelle structures that lift sebum from the scalp without stripping structural hair lipids or causing contact dermatitis.

SUBTOPIC 02 • CATIONIC CONDITIONING CHAPTER 02

Non-Silicone Detangling & Cuticle Smoothing

Mass-market conditioners coat hair in non-biodegradable dimethicone and amodimethicone, which build up over time, suffocating hair follicles and causing limpness. Renaud utilizes plant-derived cationic quaternary compounds (Behentrimonium Methosulfate derived from non-GMO colza oil) paired with Provitamin B5 (Panthenol) and cold-pressed organic jojoba. Panthenol penetrates the cortex to swell hair fibers, while jojoba esters mimic natural sebum to seal cuticles without silicone buildup.

Key Active Compounds:
Behentrimonium Methosulfate (from Colza Oil)Panthenol (Provitamin B5)Organic Jojoba OilHydrolyzed Soy ProteinOrganic Shea Butter
Target pH Range: pH 4.2 – 4.7
Rheology & Viscosity: 18,000 – 30,000 cps (Rich Cream Rinse)
Packaging & Dispensing: 8 oz, 16 oz, and Bulk Gallon Jugs
SUBTOPIC 03 • SCALP MICROBIOME CHAPTER 03

Follicular Microcirculation & Scalp Vitality Serums

Scalp health directly dictates hair density. Micro-inflammation around the follicular infundibulum and dihydrotestosterone (DHT) binding trigger follicular miniaturization. Renaud formulates leave-in scalp elixirs utilizing Rosmarinus officinalis (rosemary cineole extract, proven in clinical trials to match 2% minoxidil in hair count increases), Mentha piperita (peppermint, inducing micro-vascular vasodilation), and saw palmetto extract to block local 5-alpha-reductase.

USP <51> Antimicrobial Challenge

28-day efficacy testing verifying that clean preservative matrices actively inhibit Gram-positive (S. aureus), Gram-negative (P. aeruginosa, E. coli), and fungal pathogens (C. albicans, A. brasiliensis) without synthetic biocides.

Accelerated Thermal Stability

Hair Care Formulation & Manufacturing FAQ

Are Renaud Naturals hair care formulas safe for keratin-treated and colored hair?

Yes. Because our formulas are 100% free of sulfates (SLS/SLES) and sodium chloride, and are strictly buffered to pH 4.5–5.0, they do not swell the hair cuticle or leach color molecules.

What is the wholesale MOQ for private label hair care?

Wholesale starter orders for stock turnkey shampoo and conditioner formulas begin at just 12+ units per SKU. Custom formula R&D and salon custom scenting starts at 250+ units.

Do you offer bulk backbar gallons for salon washbasins?

Yes. We supply 1-gallon refill jugs and commercial 5-gallon pails with dispensing pumps for high-volume salons and spas.

How does Renaud Naturals formulate without dimethicone or silicones?

We use non-GMO colza-derived conditioning lipids and organic cold-pressed jojoba esters, which provide silky slip and heat protection without forming suffocating polymer buildup on the scalp.
