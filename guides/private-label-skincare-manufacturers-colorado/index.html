Starting your line in Colorado

Colorado’s rules are two filings from two agencies: a shop registration and a sales-tax license. The shop registration comes from the Colorado Office of Barber and Cosmetology Licensure (DORA), which requires one for salon and spa facilities — and a med spa offering esthetician services needs one, because the DORA bulletin covers every salon or spa facility and every licensee offering esthetician services. The bulletin does not name med spas separately, so we say that plainly rather than claim a wording that isn’t there. The sales-tax license is a Standard Retail License from the Department of Revenue — it covers wholesale sales as well, so no separate wholesale license is needed. The practitioner question is separate from both filings: the board’s site does not state a separate practitioner license for selling retail skincare, so we don’t claim one exists. One more thing, for tanning studios: the sales-tax half is the same for any Colorado business selling retail skincare, and the shop-registration half depends on the services you offer — DORA’s bulletin covers salon and spa facilities and licensees offering esthetician services.

Colorado’s elevation is also part of the brief: the air is thinner, so less UV is filtered out before it reaches skin. For every 1,000 feet above sea level, UV intensity rises by about 4% to 5% — and since much of Colorado sits at 5,000 feet or higher, Coloradans get roughly 20% to 30% more UV exposure than people at lower elevations. Who manufactures private label skincare in Colorado?

Four checked makers: Renaud Naturals in Loveland, Fifth Street Labs in Colorado Springs, OraLabs in Parker, and Midsolid in Douglas County. Each is listed above with what its own site says, checked November 2026. Trilogy Laboratories serves Colorado from Florida and is not a Colorado maker.

Do I need a shop license to sell skincare in my Colorado med spa?

Yes — a shop registration from DORA's Office of Barber and Cosmetology Licensure. It covers salon and spa facilities, and a med spa offering esthetician services needs one. The full Colorado row, with sources, is in our state-rules table.

Do I need a sales-tax license in Colorado?

Yes. A Standard Retail License from the Colorado Department of Revenue covers retail sales — and wholesale sales too, so no separate wholesale license is needed.
