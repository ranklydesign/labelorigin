Spray solutions and the DHA ladder

The professional solutions are the core of the line: the base Sass-ee Tan solution, Show-Off Competition Tan, and Colour Me Hydrated, all in gallon, quart and 8 oz formats. The confirmed DHA ladder is 8%, 8.5% and 10%, plus a no-DHA Show-Off.

Two things the table does not explain on its own. The base solution and the 8.5% retail spray tan are separate catalog listings — the base is the professional solution in gallon, quart and 8 oz; the 8.5% is a 4 oz retail spray. And Colour Me Hydrated is listed as a solution alongside the base tan and Show-Off; what it is formulated for is not published — ask for current specs.

Higher strengths are not published on sass-ee.com. If a supplier quotes you 12% or 14% DHA for SASS-EE, ask for the spec sheet — those strengths are not confirmed in the catalog. The full decision guide is choosing a spray tan solution DHA strength.

Do not shop by strength alone. The ladder is the range, not a ranking. Which strength a salon stocks depends on its clients and how it sprays — that is a conversation with the lab, not a number on a table.

Show-Off appears twice in the catalog — as the professional competition solution and as a retail product. Retail is the second sale after the spray session.

We publish no day count for how long a tan lasts. No duration is confirmed for these products, so we do not invent one. The aftercare guide is retail aftercare that extends a spray tan.

How DHA works

DHA — dihydroxyacetone — is the active ingredient behind the tan. It reacts with amino acids in the dead cells of the skin's surface layer, the stratum corneum, forming brown pigments called melanoidins. That is the Maillard reaction, the same browning chemistry as food, and it is why a spray tan develops color without sun.

Color keeps developing for hours after application. That is standard DHA behavior, not a product promise — and it is why prep and aftercare matter as much as the solution itself.
