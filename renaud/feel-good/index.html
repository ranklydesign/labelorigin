Formulation Science • Theoretical Foundation

Neuro-Sensory Modulation & Transdermal Counter-Irritation

Muscle fatigue, joint stiffness, and tension headaches respond to neuro-sensory modulation. Counter-irritants stimulate superficial thermal receptors in the skin, which transmit competing signals via A-delta and C sensory fibers to the spinal dorsal horn, effectively closing the "pain gate" (Melzack & Wall Gate Control Theory). Renaud Naturals formulates therapeutic remedies utilizing natural USP menthol crystals (activating cold TRPM8 receptors) paired with warming wintergreen and supercritical Arnica montana extract to relieve muscular stress without petroleum jellies or synthetic analgesics.

SUBTOPIC 01 • SESQUITERPENE PHARMACOLOGY CHAPTER 01

Supercritical CO2 Arnica Montana & Tissue Recovery

Arnica montana contains biologically active sesquiterpene lactones—predominantly helenalin and 11α,13-dihydrohelenalin. These compounds inhibit the activation of the transcription factor NF-κB, thereby suppressing pro-inflammatory enzyme synthesis (COX-2) and accelerating the reabsorption of subcutaneous extravasated blood (bruising and swelling). Renaud utilizes solvent-free supercritical CO2 arnica extraction, yielding a clean, ultra-potent extract with zero solvent residue.

SUBTOPIC 02 • CEPHALIC TENSION CHEMISTRY CHAPTER 02

Tension Relief & Head Space Aromatherapy Roll-Ons

Tension headaches are exacerbated by pericranial muscle spasm and vasoconstriction. Renaud formulates targeted pulse-point elixirs featuring Mentha piperita (containing >40% natural l-menthol, which relaxes cranial micro-musculature), Eucalyptus globulus (rich in 1,8-cineole, an anti-inflammatory bronchodilator that promotes oxygenation), and French Lavandula angustifolia (linalool and linalyl acetate, which modulate central nervous system GABA receptors).

Mentha Piperita (Peppermint)Eucalyptus Globulus (1,8-Cineole)Lavandula Angustifolia (Lavender)Boswellia Serrata (Frankincense)Organic Jojoba Carrier

SUBTOPIC 03 • TRANSDERMAL MINERAL THERAPY CHAPTER 03

Mineral-Rich Bath Soaks & Osmotic Muscle Recovery

Warm immersion in high-salinity bath water induces peripheral vasodilation and muscle relaxation via osmotic mineral exchange. Renaud blends genuine Dead Sea mineral salts (containing 33% magnesium chloride, potassium chloride, and bromides) with pharmaceutical-grade USP Magnesium Sulfate (Epsom salt) and certified organic whole botanical flowers (calendula, chamomile, lavender), creating a therapeutic hydrotherapy soak for spa and home recovery.

Regulatory Compliance • Quality Assurance

MoCRA, cGMP & Laboratory Testing Protocols

Every commercial batch formulated at Renaud Naturals undergoes three tiers of analytical quality control before release.

TESTING PROTOCOL 01
USP <51> Antimicrobial Challenge

28-day efficacy testing verifying that clean preservative matrices actively inhibit Gram-positive (S. aureus), Gram-negative (P. aeruginosa, E. coli), and fungal pathogens (C. albicans, A. brasiliensis) without synthetic biocides.

TESTING PROTOCOL 02
Accelerated Thermal Stability

All formulas undergo 12-week accelerated thermal aging at 40°C and 75% relative humidity (the thermodynamic equivalent of 24 months at ambient room temperature) to guarantee zero phase separation, syneresis, or color degradation.

Feel Good Formulation & Manufacturing FAQ

What active botanicals are in your therapeutic muscle balms?

Our signature muscle balms contain supercritical CO2 Arnica montana, organic cayenne fruit extract, pure natural menthol crystals, wintergreen essential oil, and organic beeswax in a cold-pressed plant oil base.

What is the wholesale MOQ for private label wellness remedies?

Wholesale starter orders for stock formulas begin at just 12+ units per SKU. Custom formula R&D and bespoke herbal compounding starts at 250+ units.

Are Renaud wellness products formulated without petroleum or mineral oil?

Yes, 100%. We never use petrolatum, mineral oil, paraffin, or synthetic preservatives. Every balm is crafted with organic food-grade plant oils and local Colorado beeswax.

Can wellness balms be packaged in hands-free twist-up sticks?

Yes. We package therapeutic balms in twist-up applicator tubes (0.5 oz, 1 oz, 2 oz) for mess-free hands-free application, as well as classic glass and aluminum jars.
