Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I submit?

Robert reviews your submission and checks the website, address, and activity. He replies to the contact email you provide within 1 business day. A listing appears once the checks pass — nothing is listed from a submission alone.

Does a listing cost anything?

A Basic listing is free. Verified and Featured pricing is by contact for now — no price is published on this site.

What do I need to submit?

A business name, a website, a contact email, at least one category, and a short message about what you make. That is everything the checks need.

Yes. Email Robert or use the contact page with the change, and the listing is updated. If the business closes or the website stops working, the listing comes down.
