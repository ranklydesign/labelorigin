Biochemistry of Sunless Tanning • The Maillard Reaction

DHA Keratin Cross-Linking & Erythrulose Anti-Orange Synergy

Sunless tanning is not a dye or pigment stain; it is a natural reaction called the Maillard Reaction. Dihydroxyacetone (DHA, a sugar-derived ingredient) reacts with free amino groups in keratin protein within the dead cells of the stratum corneum, synthesizing natural brownish pigments called melanoidins. Mass-market formulas turn brassy/orange because pure DHA reacts too quickly and unevenly. SASS-EE pairs DHA with Erythrulose—a slower-penetrating keto-sugar—and violet/green botanical color guides to guarantee a golden, true Mediterranean bronze with an even 10-to-12-day fade.

Daily Gradual Tan Extenders, Body Polishes & Barrier Creams

A successful sunless tan depends on pre-treatment and post-care:

• Oil-Free Pre-Tan Exfoliating Polish: Conventional sugar scrubs contain oils that leave a hydrophobic film, blocking DHA reaction. SASS-EE formulates an oil-free bamboo and pumice exfoliant that sloughs dead keratinocytes without leaving residual oil.

• Barrier Cream: High-density dimethicone-free wax emulsion applied to fingernails, palms, elbows, and heels to prevent unnatural dark pooling.

SASS-EE Sunless Tanning Specification FAQ

What is the history and manufacturing origin of SASS-EE?

SASS-EE is an all-natural and organic airbrush and sunless tanning brand founded in 1997 by Renaud Naturals Inc. All formulas are researched, compounded, and manufactured in-house in Loveland, Colorado, USA.

Where do I order SASS-EE wholesale versus retail?

Consumer orders are handled at sass-ee.com. This LabelOrigin page serves as the wholesale sourcing desk, salon line sheet request hub, and private-label lab inquiry portal.

What is the wholesale minimum order quantity (MOQ) for SASS-EE products?

Wholesale starter orders for stock SASS-EE spray solutions and retail mousses begin at just 12+ units per SKU. Bulk backbar gallons are available starting at 1 gallon. Custom formulation R&D for private-label brands begins at 250+ units.

How does SASS-EE help avoid the orange cast of conventional sunless tanners?

SASS-EE pairs DHA with Erythrulose and specialized botanical color guides (violet and olive base). Erythrulose reacts slowly and deeply, neutralizing orange tones for a more natural-looking fade.

Our solutions are formulated as thin, sprayable liquids for HVLP turbine systems. Ask the lab to confirm compatibility with your specific gun or booth.
