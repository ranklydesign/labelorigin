Frequently Asked Questions

About LabelOrigin
What does LabelOrigin do?

LabelOrigin is a B2B lead-generation agency. We build qualification funnels, earn organic visibility through guides and outreach, and hand pre-qualified wholesale buyers to a manufacturer's sales team.

Who is LabelOrigin for?

Manufacturers and white-label brands that sell wholesale, including skincare and personal care, supplements, apparel, and other product categories.

How are leads qualified?

Each funnel asks about business type, order volume, and program interest before a lead reaches a sales desk. Inquiries that do not meet the client's minimums don't reach the sales inbox. Not the right fit yet? We'll suggest other options or revisit when the timing's better. Every submission carries a reference ID and the buyer's own answers.

What happens when I ask for a funnel audit?

Tell us what you manufacture and who you sell to using the form on this page. We review the offer and reply with how we would approach getting you qualified wholesale buyers.

Does LabelOrigin manufacture products?

No. LabelOrigin does not manufacture anything. We build the funnels, guides, and outreach that bring buyers to manufacturers; the manufacturer formulates and ships the products.

How do I change my cookie or tracking choices?

Use the "Cookie / tracking choices" link in the footer of any page. It re-opens the consent banner so you can accept or decline again. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected and how to ask for a copy, correction, or deletion.
