Biochemistry of Sunless Tanning • The Maillard Reaction

DHA Keratin Cross-Linking & Anti-Orange Design

Sunless tanning is not a dye or pigment stain; it is a natural reaction called the Maillard Reaction. Dihydroxyacetone (DHA, a sugar-derived ingredient) reacts with free amino groups in keratin protein within the dead cells of the stratum corneum, synthesizing natural brownish pigments called melanoidins. Mass-market formulas turn brassy/orange because pure DHA reacts too quickly and unevenly. SASS-EE pairs DHA with botanical actives designed for a golden, even fade. Daily Gradual Tan Extenders, Body Polishes & Barrier Creams

• Pre-Tan Exfoliating Polish: Conventional sugar scrubs contain oils that leave a hydrophobic film, blocking DHA reaction. SASS-EE pre-tan exfoliants (Coconut Whipped, Coffee, Slough Off Gel — 8 oz) slough dead keratinocytes to prep the skin — ask for current ingredient specs.
• Daily Gradual Tan Extender: A deeply moisturizing body lotion micro-dosed with DHA — confirmed extender strengths are 2%, 3% and 5% (After-Glo, Monoi); ask for current specs — designed to extend the life of a spray tan.
