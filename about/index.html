About LabelOrigin • Westminster, Colorado

Built by someone who has installed the equipment, not just sold the idea

Three years in the field

From 2023 to September 2026, Robert worked as a Field Specialist in Technical Operations & VR at Wildlife Protection Solutions in Golden, Colorado. The work covered conservation-technology deployments spanning VR education, wildlife-monitoring cameras, cellular connectivity, solar power, and remote wildlife-deterrence systems for zoos, aquariums, field teams, and conservation partners — including support for the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Red Wolf Recovery Program in North Carolina.

He owned the customer-facing side of deployments end to end: equipment staging and configuration, installation, training, troubleshooting, documentation, and handoff. The hands-on work included building, wiring, testing, labeling, and preparing field-ready equipment — waterproof enclosures, 4G RTUs, 12V/5V power distribution, timers, audio boards, amplifiers, speakers, LEDs, solar components, and connectors — and diagnosing hardware, software, power, cellular, network, camera, and configuration issues remotely and on site, using signal measurements to judge connectivity and deployment readiness.

He also managed and supported VR systems at partner sites including Butterfly Pavilion, Downtown Aquarium, Wild Animal Sanctuary, and Phoenix Zoo — headset configuration, software, cabling, power, staff support, and guest-facing troubleshooting — and created the SOPs, setup checklists, QA procedures, quick-reference guides, training materials, inventory records, and field documentation that made handoffs work, using Airtable and QR-based tracking to improve equipment readiness.

What that background brings to lead generation

Field work rewards the same habits a good funnel needs. A deployment either works on site or it does not, and the person who installed it is the person who gets the call when it fails. That is why LabelOrigin is built around qualification before handoff: the funnel asks about business type, order volume, and program interest first, so a sales team only talks to buyers who fit — and inquiries that do not fit don't reach the sales inbox — we'll suggest other options or revisit when the timing's better. Read the full sequence on the process page.

The other carry-over is documentation. In the field, a deployment without a checklist and a handoff note is a deployment that fails the next time someone touches it. Every LabelOrigin funnel submission carries a reference ID and the buyer's own answers, so follow-up is fast and specific instead of starting from scratch.

Fewer leads, better leads. A big lead count is easy to buy. LabelOrigin optimizes for the buyers a sales team can actually close — the same way a field deployment is judged on whether the system works, not on how much equipment was shipped.

What LabelOrigin is — and is not

LabelOrigin is a B2B lead-generation agency for manufacturers and white-label brands. It builds qualification funnels, earns organic visibility through guides and outreach, and hands pre-qualified wholesale buyers to a manufacturer's sales team. The service is described on the manufacturer lead-generation page. For buyers, the private-label skincare page for med spas and the private-label skincare page for estheticians & day spas describe how to launch a line with Renaud Naturals. Its flagship client is Renaud Naturals of Loveland, Colorado, and its second brand is SASS-EE organic sunless tanning — see the Renaud Naturals funnel and the SASS-EE funnel.

LabelOrigin does not manufacture anything: the manufacturer formulates and ships the products. Growth is organic only — search-optimized guides, verified outreach, and expert quotes aimed at spas, salons, retailers, and independent brands who actually buy wholesale. Future verticals, including supplements and apparel, are in development; see the supplements and apparel pages.

Renaud Naturals

Renaud Naturals is a natural skincare manufacturer in Loveland, Colorado, founded in 1997. It formulates and manufactures all of its products in-house — natural, organic formulas, free of petrochemicals — for spas, salons, and retail stores. Its range covers facial care, body care, makeup, hair care, and wellness products, plus spa backbar for professional use. LabelOrigin is its lead-generation agency: the funnels, guides, and outreach on this site bring wholesale buyers to Renaud, and Renaud formulates and ships the products. How this site checks its facts

Robert, the operator of LabelOrigin, writes the guides on this site, drawing on his own field and e-commerce experience and on the public sources each page cites. Every figure is checked against a primary source before it is published — the official state board or revenue page for the state-rules table, the manufacturer’s own website for minimums and product facts, the published report or survey for market figures. Figures are never invented: where a number is not published anywhere, the page says so rather than guessing. Renaud Naturals’ own minimums, for example, are confirmed in writing on request and are not published here.

If you spot an error, write in through the contact page and say which page and which figure. Verified corrections are fixed and the page’s updated date is changed.
