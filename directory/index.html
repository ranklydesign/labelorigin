Frequently Asked Questions

How does a manufacturer get listed?

Submit the Get listed form with the business name, website, contact email, and categories. Robert checks the business is real — a real website, a real address, an active business — and we reply by email, usually within a business day. A listing appears once the checks pass.

What does the Checked badge mean?

Checked means LabelOrigin checked the business before listing it: the website loads, the address is real, and the business is active. It is a check that the business exists, not a review of product quality or a promise about any order.

What does Featured cost?

Featured pricing is by contact for now — no price is published on this site. Featured listings get top placement in their category, and their link is marked as sponsored because the placement is paid.

Is a Basic listing really free?

Yes. A Basic listing costs nothing and stays listed as long as the business is real and the details stay current. Basic listings do not carry the Checked badge and do not get top placement.
