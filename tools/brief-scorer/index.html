Score your brief

Nothing you paste leaves your browser. This tool makes no network calls. Your brief is checked on your device only, and it is never shown back to you on this page or sent anywhere.

Your score will appear here after you score your brief. Found and missing fields are listed below the score, with guidance for every missing field.

How the scoring works

The check is deliberately simple and transparent. Your pasted text is matched case-insensitively against the words listed for each field. A field counts as found if any of its words appears in your brief. A field can match on one word or several; the score is the number of fields found out of 26. No judgement is made about whether a field is filled in well — only whether it is mentioned at all — so use the missing-field guidance below the score to fill each gap properly.

Before you send the brief

Send the same completed brief to each manufacturer, and ask for every quote itemized, in writing, on the same basis — same products, same units, same packaging — or the totals will mislead you. Frequently asked questions

Does this tool send my brief anywhere?

No. The scoring runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you paste is sent anywhere or stored. You can check a brief, close the page, and nothing about it leaves your device.

Is a high score a quote?

No. A high score means your brief covers the fields a manufacturer needs to quote accurately. It is not a price, and every figure still comes from the manufacturer in an itemized quote.
