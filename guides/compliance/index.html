Frequently Asked Questions

Compliance hub
Do I need a licence to sell skincare in my spa?

In most states, yes — the licence that covers retail skincare varies by state, and a few states license the premises rather than the shop. The state-rules table lists the licensing body, whether a separate retail licence is needed, and the sales-tax permit for every state and the District of Columbia, each row sourced to the official state page.

What does MoCRA require of a brand owner?

The brand owner is the responsible person: facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation, adverse-event records, and labelling. The MoCRA guides split what sits with you from what the manufacturer handles, and the MoCRA checklist walks through each duty.

What has to be on the label?

Five FDA elements plus the MoCRA contact line, ingredients in INCI order, and net contents — and any claim that changes the rules. The label guide walks through each element and the claims that move a product into drug territory.

Do I need insurance to sell my own line?

Product liability insurance covers claims that a product caused harm, and some marketplaces and retailers require it before they will stock you. The insurance guide explains what it covers, what it costs, and where it is required.
