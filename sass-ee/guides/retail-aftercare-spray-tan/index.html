Retail aftercare that extends a spray tan

Aftercare is where a spray tan becomes a retail sale. SASS-EE's extenders are daily lotions with a small amount of DHA, made to be sold after the appointment. This guide lists the confirmed aftercare range — and makes no promises about how long a tan lasts.

What extenders are

Extenders are daily lotions with a small amount of DHA. The client uses them between appointments to maintain color — a little DHA each day, on top of the spray session, instead of a second full application. They are aftercare, not a replacement for the appointment itself.

The confirmed extenders are After-Glo Crème at 2% DHA and Monoi Tan Extender at 3% and 5% DHA, all in 8 oz, with back-bar gallons available. That is the whole confirmed extender range: two products, three strengths.

Retail crèmes, gels and sprays

Beyond extenders, the retail side is crèmes, gels and sprays at 8% and 10% DHA in formats from 1 to 8 oz across the retail line, plus the 8.5% Sass-ee Spray Tan in 4 oz. Confirmed names: the 8% retail spray tan, sunless gel and face crème; Show-Off, Monoi Sunless Crème and Seriously Addictive at 10%. There is no mousse or foam product in the catalog.

The full catalog is on the SASS-EE product overview. The DHA decision is covered in choosing a spray tan solution DHA strength.

Selling aftercare in the treatment room

Aftercare sells at the appointment, not on the shelf. The client is standing in front of you with fresh color — that is the moment to explain daily use and hand them the bottle. Three things make it work:

  • Offer it every time. Aftercare is part of the service, not an upsell.
  • Explain daily use plainly. A small amount of DHA each day maintains the color from the appointment. No day counts, no guarantees — just the routine.
  • Stock two strengths to start. After-Glo at 2% and one Monoi strength — 3% for a lighter step up, 5% for a stronger one. Start narrow and expand from sales.

Retail aftercare is repeat revenue without an appointment slot: no artist time, just a bottle that leaves with the client.
