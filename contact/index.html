What to tell us

A few sentences is enough: what you manufacture, who you sell to, and what you need — a new funnel, a sample kit for your shortlist, a correction to a page on this site, or something else. A link to your website helps, and so does anything your buyers already ask you for.

What happens after you write in

Your message goes to Robert, who reviews it — we reply by email, usually within a business day, to the work email you provide. Every submission carries a reference ID, so if you have not heard back by then, email ranklydesign@gmail.com directly and mention the reference — we will get back to you. There is no obligation in writing in; a reply is a conversation, not a contract.
