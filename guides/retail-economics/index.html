Frequently Asked Questions

Retail economics hub
How much does it cost to start a skincare line?

A stock-formula first run commonly lands between roughly $1,500 and $10,000; a custom-formulation launch commonly starts around $5,000 to $25,000 and up. The cost guides break both bands into their real buckets — and the five costs founders forget.

Private label or reselling — which earns more per shelf slot?

It depends on commission, turn rate, and your cost per unit. The margins guide runs the math both ways, with worked examples, so you can compare your own numbers instead of guessing.

When does the line pay for itself?

Once cumulative margin covers the first-run cost. The retail margin calculator runs your unit cost, retail price, monthly units, and staff commission and gives you the payback in units.
