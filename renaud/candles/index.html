Candles Specification FAQ

What wax and wick materials do you use for private label candles?

We use 100% pure US-grown soy wax and pure local beeswax. Our wicks are constructed of braided unbleached cotton with paper cores, 100% free of lead and heavy metals.

What candle vessel sizes and styles do you offer?

We offer classic 8 oz amber glass jars, frosted tumblers, clear apothecary jars with cork lids, and 4 oz / 8 oz seamless travel tins.

What is the MOQ for private label candles?

Stock private-label candle scents start at 12+ units per SKU. Custom scent development and branded vessel filling starts at 250+ units.

Do your candles contain synthetic fragrances or phthalates?

No. Every candle poured at Renaud Naturals is 100% phthalate-free and scented with authentic pure essential oils and natural botanical aromatics.
