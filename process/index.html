What happens after you submit a funnel

Every LabelOrigin funnel works the same way: you answer four short steps, your answers are checked against the client's minimums, and either your submission goes to the client's sales team with a reference ID — or you go to a waitlist instead of the sales inbox. Nothing is sold to you in between, and no one on a sales desk sees your details until you have qualified.

The four steps on every funnel

The live funnels — Renaud Naturals and SASS-EE — each run as a four-step form that takes under a minute. The steps are the same on both:

  1. Tell us about your business. What best describes your operation — a day spa or medical spa, a massage or esthetics studio, a sunless tanning salon, a retail storefront, or a brand founder launching a line.
  2. Tell us your volume. Your estimated initial order run. This is the qualification gate: the funnel separates buyers who meet the client's minimums from those who do not, before anyone reaches a sales desk.
  3. Choose your program. Which product line or program interests you — facial care, body care, massage and backbar, sunless tanning, or a full custom formulation, depending on the funnel.
  4. Leave your details. Your name, business name, work email, and phone number, plus any specific requirements. Every submission carries a reference ID and your own answers, so follow-up is fast and specific.

What happens after you submit

There are two outcomes, decided by the volume step — not by a salesperson's judgment call:

OutcomeWhat happens
You qualifyYour submission is delivered to LabelOrigin and forwarded to the client's sales team, with your reference ID and the answers you gave. The sales team follows up from there — your details are not passed on until this point.
Below the minimumYou are routed to a waitlist instead of the sales inbox. You leave an email and a note about the line you are planning, and you are notified when a program opens that fits smaller volumes. The confirmation points you to the client's retail site if you only need single units.

The waitlist is not a dead end and it is not a sales queue in disguise: under-minimum buyers stay on it for future programs, and the funnel is tuned from lead and order feedback over time. That is also why the funnels are isolated pages with no site menu to wander off to — one page, one goal, and every visitor stays focused on the one offer.

What you receive

A qualified submission gets a reference ID on screen the moment it is sent, so you have something concrete to quote in any follow-up. After that, the client's catalog and line sheets and the links to order sample evaluation units are delivered to the inbox you provided. The exact contents depend on the funnel — the Renaud funnel covers line sheets and sample kits, the SASS-EE funnel covers wholesale line sheets and sample kits for sunless products — but the shape is the same: reference ID first, catalog and samples next, then the sales conversation.

Response times, stated honestly

Two different clocks apply, and they should not be confused:

  • Buyer funnel submissions: on the Renaud Naturals and SASS-EE funnels, the confirmation screen states the client's team reviews submissions within 1 business day. That is the client's review time, stated on the funnel itself — not a LabelOrigin promise.
  • Funnel-audit inquiries from manufacturers: LabelOrigin publishes no general reply-time promise. The home page says only that we review your offer and reply with how we would approach getting you qualified wholesale buyers. It does not state when. If you need a reply by a specific date, say so in your message and we will tell you plainly whether that is possible.

No reply-time number is promised here that the site does not already state. If a faster or firmer turnaround is needed, that is a decision for the operator to confirm — not something this page will invent.

For manufacturers: the funnel-audit path

If you manufacture for other brands and want wholesale leads instead of window-shoppers, the path starts on the home page contact form: tell us what you manufacture and who you sell to. We review the offer and reply with how we would approach getting you qualified wholesale buyers. That review is the whole audit — there is no charge described anywhere on this site for asking, and no pricing is published on labelorigin.com.

The person doing that review is the same person who runs the agency. Read the background on the about page.

How a funnel is built

The home page describes the build in four steps, and the process page follows the same shape:

  1. Drive targeted traffic. Search-optimized guides and verified outreach send buyers to one isolated landing page — the buyer guides and the cost guide are the main traffic earners.
  2. Qualify in under a minute. Business type, volume, and interest are captured step by step before anyone reaches the client's team.
  3. Route to the sales desk. Viable leads land with the client's team with a reference ID and the buyer's own answers. Everyone else is parked on a waitlist.
  4. Refine and follow up. The funnel is tuned from lead and order feedback, and under-minimum buyers stay on the waitlist for future programs.
