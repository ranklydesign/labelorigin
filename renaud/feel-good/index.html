Made in-house since 1997

Renaud Naturals formulates and manufactures in-house in Loveland, Colorado, and makes its products in small batches, fresh to order. It ships across the USA and abroad. Renaud Naturals states that its products are free of mineral oils, lanolin, parabens, PABA, alcohol, petrochemicals, sodium lauryl sulfate and formaldehyde, and are never tested on animals.

Feel Good private label FAQ

Can I sell Renaud Naturals products under my own brand?

Yes. Renaud Naturals says you can use its products as they are with your own labels, and that single products or full lines can be made to your specifications.

Where are the products made?

Renaud Naturals formulates and manufactures in-house at its facility at 101 East 5th Street in Loveland, Colorado, and has done so since 1997.

What are the minimum order quantities?

Minimums depend on the product, formula and packaging, and pricing is quoted per project. Contact Renaud Naturals for current terms.

Does Renaud Naturals test on animals?

No. Renaud Naturals states that it never tests on animals.
