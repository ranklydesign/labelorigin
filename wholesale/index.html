What you get

Your own label on formulas made for the treatment room. Renaud Naturals makes private-label skincare two ways: stock formulas — existing, finished formulas sold under your own brand, packaging, and labels — and custom formulation, a formula developed to your specification. The range covers facial care, body care, makeup, hair care, and wellness products, plus spa backbar for professional use.

The formulas come from Renaud Naturals, a natural skincare manufacturer in Loveland, Colorado, formulating and manufacturing all of its products in-house since 1997. Production is in small batches, fresh to order, made in the USA, and shipped across the USA and abroad. See the Renaud Naturals funnel for the full catalog, or the private-label page for med spas for what a line built this way looks like, product by product.

Put your name on it. Your clients trust you, not a brand you resell — the line carries your name, your protocols, and your recommendations.

How ordering works

It starts with your treatments: tell us what you perform most and which products the line should cover. LabelOrigin’s funnel asks about business type, order volume, and program interest first, so the conversation starts with buyers who fit — and inquiries that do not fit go to a waitlist instead of the sales inbox.

Every funnel submission carries a reference ID and your own answers, so follow-up is fast and specific. We reply with the current minimums and timing for your shortlist in writing, and send the sample kit. LabelOrigin does not manufacture anything — it builds the funnels, guides, and outreach that bring wholesale buyers to manufacturers; Renaud Naturals formulates and ships the products.

Sample kits first

Request a sample kit before you commit to any product, any formula, or any manufacturer. Try the formulas in your treatment room, on skin, over days — the way your clients will actually use them. Have your providers evaluate samples specifically on post-procedure suitability: skin recovering from a procedure needs gentler, simpler formulas than daily maintenance products.

Ask about sample kit options and pricing in the form below. There is no obligation to order after trying the samples. A manufacturer that is slow or vague at the sample stage will not get faster after you have paid.

Minimums and terms

Low minimums, sized to a practice rather than a national brand. The per-SKU minimum depends on the formula and packaging you choose, so LabelOrigin does not publish a number. Ask in the sample-request form below and you will get the current per-SKU minimums for your shortlist in writing before you commit to anything — ask for current terms, and confirm them in writing.

Before your first retail sale, check the permission side too: see the state-by-state rules for selling skincare in a med spa — the license and sales-tax requirements for your state, verified against official state sources, with unverified entries marked.
