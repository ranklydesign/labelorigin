What does a private-label skincare line for a med spa look like?

A med-spa line is usually built around the treatment menu: a gentle cleanser and barrier moisturizer for post-procedure care, a daily serum for maintenance between visits, sun protection, and one or two targeted products matched to your most-booked services. The products are sold as aftercare first and retail second, recommended in the consultation and dispensed at the front desk.

Because your clients are already ingredient-literate, the line lives or dies on transparency: full ingredient lists, plain explanations of what each product does, and no borrowed clinical language you cannot support. Clients who trust the clinic trust the products; clients who catch an overstatement stop trusting both. The longer educational version of this page — positioning, the five steps, and what to ask a manufacturer — is the private-label guide for med spas.

Objections, answered

How long does it take?

Stock formulas move faster than custom formulation, which needs development time and usually a second sample round. We do not quote a turnaround on this page because it depends on the route you choose and the formulas on your shortlist. Ask in the sample-request form and we will reply with the current timing for your line.

What about ingredients and compliance?

Ask for full ingredient disclosure — complete lists for every formula, including fragrance components — before you approve anything. Ask what testing is included in the quote and what is billed separately: stability, preservative-effectiveness, and any safety testing. And confirm who checks the label, and whether regulatory review is part of the service or an extra. The label review matters: labeling obligations generally sit with you as the brand owner.

Who handles MoCRA?

MoCRA — the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act of 2022 — expanded the FDA's authority over cosmetics, including facility registration, product listing, safety substantiation, adverse event reporting, and good manufacturing practice. The "responsible person" under MoCRA is the manufacturer, packer, or distributor whose name appears on the product label — for a private-label brand, that is usually you, the brand owner, not the contract manufacturer. The duties cannot be handed off by contract alone, but the practical work can be assigned: a manufacturer can register its facility, list products, and keep records. Confirm in writing who does what, and verify it. Our MoCRA guide for brand owners explains the duties in plain terms.

Your providers are the quality filter. If the clinicians performing the treatments would not recommend a product to their own patients, it does not belong in your line — no matter how good the margin looks on paper.

What does a med-spa private-label line start with?

The aftercare your treatments already need: a gentle cleanser and barrier moisturizer for post-procedure care, a daily serum, and sun protection — usually four to six products tied to your most-booked services. Start from your top three services and work backward to the aftercare each one needs.

Who handles MoCRA compliance?

Can I try the products before I commit?
