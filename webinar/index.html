What you will learn

The webinar follows the five published guides, in the same order and with the same limits: general industry patterns, labeled as such, so you can compare real quotes fairly when they arrive.

  • The three paths — stock formulas, semi-custom adjustments, and custom formulation, compared on speed, cost, control, and minimums, including who owns a custom formula.
  • Launch costs — the seven cost buckets in every quote, the worksheet for pricing your own launch, and the costs founders most often forget.
  • Choosing a manufacturer — fit before price, the samples process, and a weighted scorecard for comparing candidates on the same brief.
  • MoCRA duties — what the brand owner, as the name on the label, remains responsible for, and the row-by-row split with the manufacturer.
  • Minimums — what minimum order quantities mean, why they vary, and the math that sizes a first run to what you can sell in a few months.

You can also run the worksheet numbers yourself with the launch cost calculator, which uses the same published cost bands as the cost guide.
