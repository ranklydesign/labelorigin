How to read it

The published first-run bands in our cost guide are roughly $1,500–$10,000 for a stock-formula launch and $5,000–$25,000+ for a custom-formulation launch. The band check above compares your estimate against the band for your path. If your range sits inside the band, your plan looks like a typical first launch. If it straddles the band, one end of your plan is outside the usual range — usually because your units are small (fixed costs dominate) or your path is custom (development adds thousands before production starts).

The range is wide on purpose. Product fill alone runs from $1.50 to $12.00 per unit across product types, and packaging from $0.50 to $5.00 per unit — so the same launch can legitimately cost several times more or less depending on what you make and how you pack it. The low end assumes the cheapest published figures on every bucket at once; the high end assumes the most expensive. Most real quotes land somewhere inside, not at either edge.

Three things move the total most. First, units per product: nearly every cost is priced per run or per unit, and minimums decide your floor. Second, the path: custom formulation adds development fees of $2,000 to $15,000+ per formula before a single unit is filled, and two sample rounds instead of one. Third, the number of products: development and testing are priced per formula, so each extra SKU adds a full round of both.

Not a quote. Every figure above is a general industry pattern, not a price from any manufacturer. Use the budget to judge real quotes, then get itemized quotes on the same basis — same products, units, and packaging — before committing to a launch size.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to start a skincare line?

Starting with stock (private-label) formulas is usually the cheapest route, because the formula already exists and you are paying mainly for product, packaging, and labels. Custom formulation costs more because development, sampling, and testing are added on top.

Can I start a skincare line with $1,000?

It is possible only in narrow cases — for example, one stock product, a small run, and simple packaging — and only if the manufacturer's minimums allow it. Most founders planning two or three products should expect a first-launch budget in the low thousands at minimum, before marketing.
