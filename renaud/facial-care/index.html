Formulation Science • Theoretical Foundation

Epidermal Physiology & Transdermal Formulation Mechanics

The stratum corneum operates as a brick-and-mortar lipid barrier consisting of 50% ceramides, 25% cholesterol, and 15% free fatty acids. Formulating high-performance clean facial care requires matching the skin’s natural lipid crystalline phase without relying on occlusive petro-chemicals, synthetic silicones (dimethicone, cyclopentasiloxane), or toxic emulsifiers. At Renaud Naturals, all facial emulsions are buffered between pH 4.8 and 5.5—the precise acid mantle range required to preserve resident skin microflora and prevent microbial pathogen colonization.

SUBTOPIC 01 • ACTIVE SERUMS CHAPTER 01

Transdermal Peptide & Hyaluronic Acid Delivery Systems

Peptides are polar hydrophilic oligomers that naturally struggle to penetrate the hydrophobic stratum corneum. Renaud utilizes biomimetic lipopeptide sequences (such as Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1 and Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7) conjugated with lipophilic palmitic acid chains to facilitate trans-epidermal flux. We combine low-molecular-weight sodium hyaluronate (50 kDa) for deep cellular hydration with high-molecular-weight hyaluronic acid (1.5 MDa) to create a non-tacky, breathable surface moisture shield.

Key Active Compounds:
Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7Sodium Hyaluronate (Multi-Weight)Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)Botanical Squalane
Target pH Range: pH 5.0 – 5.4
Rheology & Viscosity: 1,200 – 2,500 cps (Liquid Serum)
Packaging & Dispensing: 1 oz (30ml) Frosted Amber Glass with Medical Dropper or Airless Vacuum Pump
SUBTOPIC 02 • ANTIOXIDANT CHEMISTRY CHAPTER 02

Vitamin C Stabilization & Radical Quenching Cascades

Traditional L-Ascorbic acid oxidizes rapidly into dehydroascorbic acid when exposed to air, light, or water, turning brown and generating pro-oxidant free radicals. Renaud Naturals formulates with advanced stabilized derivatives: Tetrahexyldecyl (THD) Ascorbate (a lipid-soluble ester that penetrates through cellular membranes 50x more effectively than standard ascorbic acid) and Magnesium Ascorbyl Phosphate (MAP), protected in an antioxidant network with Ferulic Acid and d-alpha-tocopherol (natural Vitamin E).

Key Active Compounds:
Tetrahexyldecyl AscorbateMagnesium Ascorbyl PhosphateFerulic AcidOrganic Rosehip Fruit OilTocopherol
Target pH Range: pH 5.5 – 6.0 (Neutral Skin pH)
Rheology & Viscosity: 1,500 – 3,000 cps
Packaging & Dispensing: 1 oz Miron Violet or Opaque Ceramic Airless Pump to block 100% photolytic degradation
SUBTOPIC 03 • LIPID SCIENCE CHAPTER 03

Ceramide Bilayer Emulsions & Barrier Repair Moisturizers

Impaired barrier function leads to elevated transepidermal water loss (TEWL) and inflammatory cytokine release. Renaud’s moisturizers utilize cold-pressed organic Simmondsia chinensis (jojoba wax esters, structurally identical to human sebum), cold-pressed Borago officinalis (containing 24% gamma-linolenic acid), and plant-derived phytosterols. Emulsification is achieved using Olivem 1000 (cetearyl olivate and sorbitan olivate), creating liquid crystal structures that fuse with damaged skin lipids.

Key Active Compounds:
Cetearyl Olivate / Sorbitan OlivateOrganic Jojoba OilBorage Seed OilOlive-Derived SqualaneOrganic Calendula Extract
Target pH Range: pH 5.2 – 5.6
Rheology & Viscosity: 15,000 – 35,000 cps (Rich Cream)
Packaging & Dispensing: 1.7 oz (50ml) Glass Jars with Inner Seal Discs or Airless Cosmetic Treatment Pumps
SUBTOPIC 04 • SURFACTANT SCIENCE CHAPTER 04

Non-Stripping Cleanser Chemistry & Amino Acid Glucosides

Conventional cleansers employ harsh anionic surfactants (SLS, SLES) that denature structural keratin proteins and strip protective intercellular lipids, leaving the skin tight and sensitized. Renaud formulates sulfate-free cleansers using ultra-mild alkyl polyglucosides (Coco Glucoside, Decyl Glucoside) paired with sodium cocoyl hydrolyzed soy protein. These generate dense, micro-fine foam with low critical micelle concentration, lifting oil and environmental debris without lipid stripping.

Key Active Compounds:
Decyl GlucosideCoco GlucosideOrganic Green Tea HydrosolOrganic German ChamomileVegetable Glycerin
Target pH Range: pH 5.0 – 5.5
Rheology & Viscosity: 3,000 – 6,000 cps (Silky Gel) or Low-Viscosity Foam Dispenser
Packaging & Dispensing: 4 oz & 8 oz Recyclable PET/Glass Bottles with Treatment Pump or Foamer Head
SUBTOPIC 05 • DELICATE ORBITAL TISSUE CHAPTER 05

Ocular Microcirculation & Anti-Fatigue Eye Balms

Periorbital skin is 0.5mm thick—less than one-third the thickness of the rest of the face—and lacks oil glands, making it exceptionally vulnerable to vascular pooling (dark circles) and lymphatic edema (puffiness). Renaud formulates eye balms with green coffee seed caffeine (vasoconstrictor), wildcrafted Arnica montana (flavonoids that resolve micro-bruising), and plant squalane designed with a non-migrating lipid profile to prevent ocular irritation.

Regulatory Compliance • Quality Assurance

MoCRA, cGMP & Laboratory Testing Protocols

Every commercial batch formulated at Renaud Naturals undergoes three tiers of analytical quality control before release.

TESTING PROTOCOL 01
USP <51> Antimicrobial Challenge

28-day efficacy testing verifying that clean preservative matrices actively inhibit Gram-positive (S. aureus), Gram-negative (P. aeruginosa, E. coli), and fungal pathogens (C. albicans, A. brasiliensis) without synthetic biocides.

TESTING PROTOCOL 02
Accelerated Thermal Stability

All formulas undergo 12-week accelerated thermal aging at 40°C and 75% relative humidity (the thermodynamic equivalent of 24 months at ambient room temperature) to guarantee zero phase separation, syneresis, or color degradation.

Facial Care Formulation & Manufacturing FAQ

What is the exact wholesale MOQ for private label facial care at Renaud Naturals?

Wholesale minimum order quantity for our proven turnkey stock formulas is just 12+ units per SKU. For bespoke custom R&D formulations created from scratch by our chemistry team, the minimum order quantity is 250+ units.

How does Renaud Naturals preserve clean facial formulas without parabens or phenoxyethanol?

We use synergistic hurdle technology: multi-functional botanical glycols (pentylene glycol derived from sugarcane), caprylyl glycol, and radish root ferment filtrate (Leuconostoc) buffered with food-grade potassium sorbate at pH 4.8–5.2. All formulas pass strict USP <51> antimicrobial challenge testing.

How long does custom formula R&D and sample turnaround take?

Lab sample evaluation kits ($3 to $5 each) dispatch within 2 to 3 business days. Custom formula R&D iterations typically require 2 weeks for initial bench batches and stability verification.

What are the FDA MoCRA compliance responsibilities for facial brand founders?

Under the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act (MoCRA), brand owners must submit cosmetic product listings to FDA Direct and maintain safety substantiation files. Renaud Naturals is a registered manufacturing facility that provides full batch lot tracing, COAs, and stability dossiers to satisfy all MoCRA compliance audits.
