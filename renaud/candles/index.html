SUBTOPIC 01 • LIPID CRYSTALLIZATION CHAPTER 01

Soy & Beeswax Melting Curves & Frosting Prevention

Soy wax exhibits complex polymorphic crystallization (alpha, beta-prime, and stable beta crystals). Rapid temperature drops cause polymorphic phase shifts that manifest as surface "frosting" or cracking. Renaud utilizes a dual-lipid system blending low-melt soy wax (115°F–120°F) with high-melt Colorado beeswax (144°F–147°F). Beeswax acts as a natural crystal modifier, raising the overall hardness, eliminating paraffin additives, and dramatically extending total burn duration.

SUBTOPIC 02 • COMBUSTION PHYSICS CHAPTER 02

Wick Sizing, Capillary Draw & Melt Pool Dynamics

A candle operates as a micro-chemical refinery: liquid wax is drawn up the wick by capillary action, vaporized by the heat of the flame, and oxidized with atmospheric oxygen. If the wick is undersized, the candle "tunnels," leaving wasted wax on the container walls. If oversized, the flame consumes wax faster than it can vaporize, producing soot and high carbon monoxide. Renaud uses lead-free, unbleached braided cotton wicks with paper filament cores (ECO / CD series), precisely matched to container diameter for a full edge-to-edge melt pool within 2 hours.

Key Active Compounds:
Braided Unbleached Cotton WicksPaper Filament Core (Lead-Free)High-Density Wax Matrix
Target pH Range: Solid Phase
Rheology & Viscosity: Liquid in Melt Pool: ~10 cps
Packaging & Dispensing: Calibrated to Jar Internal Diameters of 2.75" to 3.25"
SUBTOPIC 03 • THERMAL VOLATILITY CHAPTER 03

Essential Oil Flash Points & Hot Scent Throw

Essential oils have distinct flash points (e.g. sweet orange at 115°F, lavender at 150°F, cedarwood at 200°F). Adding volatile oils into molten wax at excessive temperatures causes instant thermal flash-off, destroying the aroma before the candle is poured. Renaud incorporates 8% to 10% pure essential oil fragrance loads at exact thermal windows (135°F–140°F), allowing the essential oil molecules to bond completely with the lipid matrix without curdling or burning.

Key Active Compounds:
Pure Essential Oils (Lavender, Orange, Cedarwood, Eucalyptus, Fir Needle)Plant-Based Aromatics
Target pH Range: Anhydrous
Rheology & Viscosity: Controlled Thermal Pour
Packaging & Dispensing: Amber Glass to Prevent Photolytic Essential Oil Degradation
