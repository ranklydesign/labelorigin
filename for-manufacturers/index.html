What we do for manufacturers

Most manufacturers do not have a lead problem — they have a filtering problem. Inquiries arrive from everywhere: consumers looking for one bottle, resellers with no storefront, brands that cannot meet a minimum, and the occasional buyer who is exactly right. Sorting that pile by hand eats the hours that could go to production, sampling, and the buyers worth keeping.

LabelOrigin replaces the pile with a funnel. We build a dedicated page for your products that asks each visitor the questions that matter — what kind of business they run, how much they order, and which program they are interested in — before they ever reach your team. The buyers who fit arrive with their answers attached and a reference ID on the submission, so follow-up starts from real information instead of a cold hello.

The traffic comes from search-optimized guides and direct outreach aimed at the buyers you want — med spas, salons, retailers, and independent brands — so the funnel is found by people already looking for a manufacturer. Qualified leads land in your inbox with a reference ID and the answers they gave. Under-minimum inquiries don’t reach your inbox.

One honest note. LabelOrigin is a young agency, and Renaud Naturals is the flagship client the work is built around. We do not claim a roster of paying clients or results we have not earned. What we can show is the funnel, the guides behind it, and the qualification logic that keeps a sales desk talking to buyers who fit.

Who fits

This is built for US skincare and cosmetics manufacturers that sell wholesale and make in small batches — typically a small, founder-led team where the founder still touches production, samples, and customer conversations, and where every hour spent chasing the wrong lead is an hour not spent making product.

The proof case is Renaud Naturals, LabelOrigin’s flagship client: a natural skincare manufacturer in Loveland, Colorado, formulating and manufacturing all of its products in-house since 1997 — small batches, fresh to order, made in the USA, and shipped across the USA and abroad, offering private-label stock formulas and custom formulation. You can see the funnel itself at labelorigin.com/renaud/, and the full service behind it on the manufacturer lead generation page.

If you already sell through distributors, this does not replace them. It adds a direct channel of buyers who have already said what they want, before anyone reaches your team.

That skincare work is the proof case. The same funnel now covers five more areas — supplements, apparel, hemp and CBD, packaging, and food and beverage co-packing — each with its own section below.

What we don’t do

Three things, stated plainly so nobody wastes an inquiry. First, we do not manufacture anything. We build the funnels, guides, and outreach that bring buyers to manufacturers; the manufacturer formulates and ships the products.

Second, we do not resell product. LabelOrigin never buys, prices, or ships your product — you quote and fulfill every order, and the buyer relationship stays yours.

Third, we do not run your sales process for you. We hand you buyers who fit, with their answers and a reference ID attached, and your team takes the conversation from there. The funnel filters; it does not close.
