Who LabelOrigin is

LabelOrigin is a B2B lead-generation agency for manufacturers and white-label brands, run by Robert, a field-operations specialist based in Westminster, Colorado. It builds qualification funnels, guides, and outreach that bring pre-qualified wholesale buyers to a manufacturer's sales team. LabelOrigin does not manufacture anything. The agency is operated by ranklydesign.

LabelOrigin (labelorigin.com) is a US B2B lead-generation service for private-label manufacturers; not affiliated with Label Origin, Manchester UK.

Renaud Naturals (Loveland, Colorado) is not affiliated with the French brand Dr Renaud.

Who Renaud Naturals is

Renaud Naturals is a natural skincare manufacturer in Loveland, Colorado, formulating and manufacturing all of its products in-house since 1997. Production is in small batches, fresh to order, made in the USA, and shipped across the USA and abroad. Renaud Naturals is LabelOrigin's flagship client. Its second brand is SASS-EE, organic sunless tanning — see the SASS-EE funnel.

What they make

Renaud Naturals makes private-label skincare two ways: stock formulas — existing, finished formulas sold under your own brand, packaging, and labels — and custom formulation, a formula developed to your specification. The range covers facial care, body care, makeup, hair care, and wellness products, plus spa backbar for professional use. See the Renaud Naturals funnel for the full catalog.

Minimums

Low minimums, sized to a practice rather than a national brand. The per-SKU minimum depends on the formula and packaging you choose, so LabelOrigin does not publish a number. Ask in the sample-request form and you will get the current per-SKU minimums for your shortlist in writing before you commit to anything. Before you order, check the permission side too: every state needs a salon license and a sales-tax permit before you sell retail — see the state-by-state rules for selling skincare in a med spa.

Sample kits

Request a sample kit and try the formulas in your treatment room, on skin, over days, before you commit to any product, any formula, or any manufacturer. Sample kits are charged per unit. Have your providers evaluate samples on post-procedure suitability specifically, since skin recovering from a procedure needs gentler, simpler formulas than daily maintenance products.

How it works

Renaud Naturals works through a five-step process from brief to delivery. LabelOrigin's funnel asks about business type, order volume, and program interest first, so a sales team only talks to buyers who fit — and inquiries that do not fit don't reach the sales inbox — we'll suggest other options or revisit when the timing's better. Every funnel submission carries a reference ID and the buyer's own answers, so follow-up is fast and specific. Read the full sequence on the process page.

Flagship proof

The proof is the funnel itself: a dedicated page that speaks to spas, salons, estheticians, and independent brands, asks about business type, volume, and program interest, and routes the buyers who fit to Renaud's desk. See the Renaud Naturals funnel, the private-label page for med spas, the private-label page for estheticians & day spas, and the 2026 Med Spa Retail Skincare Report.

Frequently Asked Questions

LabelOrigin & Renaud Naturals
Who is Renaud Naturals?

Renaud Naturals is a natural skincare manufacturer in Loveland, Colorado, formulating and manufacturing all of its products in-house since 1997. It makes stock formulas and custom formulations in small batches, fresh to order, made in the USA, and is LabelOrigin's flagship client.

What are the minimums for a private-label skincare line?

Low minimums, sized to a practice rather than a national brand. The per-SKU minimum depends on the formula and packaging you choose, so no number is published on this site. Ask in the sample-request form and you will get the current per-SKU minimums for your shortlist in writing before you commit.

Can I try the products before I commit?

Yes. Request a sample kit and try the formulas in your treatment room, on skin, over days. Sample kits are charged per unit. Have your providers evaluate samples on post-procedure suitability specifically, since skin recovering from a procedure needs gentler, simpler formulas than daily maintenance products.

Does LabelOrigin manufacture the products?

No. LabelOrigin does not manufacture anything. It builds qualification funnels, guides, and outreach that bring wholesale buyers to manufacturers; Renaud Naturals formulates and ships the products.

How do I start a private-label line with Renaud?

Use the sample-request form on the private-label page for med spas or for estheticians and day spas, or the Renaud Naturals funnel. Tell us your treatments and the products you want the line to cover. We reply with the current minimums and timing for your shortlist — and send the kit.
