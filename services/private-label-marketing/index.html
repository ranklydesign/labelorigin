What private-label marketing means here

A private-label manufacturer makes products that other brands sell under their own name. The manufacturer handles formulation, filling, packaging, and shipping. The marketing job is different: reaching the people who buy wholesale — founders launching a brand, spa and salon owners stocking treatment rooms, retailers filling shelves, distributors supplying a region — and getting them to raise a hand with real details about their business.

That is the whole service. LabelOrigin builds one dedicated funnel for your offer, earns organic visibility through search-optimized guides and verified outreach, asks each inquiry about business type, order volume, and program interest, and hands the ones that fit to your sales desk with a reference ID and the buyer's own answers. Inquiries that do not meet your minimums don't reach your inbox. Nothing here is about consumer traffic: every page, guide, and outreach message is written for the buyer who orders in volume.

If you are a brand looking for a manufacturer rather than a manufacturer looking for buyers, start with our guide on how to find wholesale buyers and our manufacturer lead-generation service — both explain the buyer side of the same relationship.

Three verticals, three different buyers

Private label is not one market. A spa owner buying a cleanser, a retailer ordering a supplement, and a brand sourcing a hoodie are different buyers with different questions and different places they look. The funnel changes with the vertical, even when the mechanics — attract, qualify, route — stay the same.

Skincare and personal care

Buyers here are spa and med-spa owners, estheticians, salon owners, independent brands, and retailers. They ask about stock formulas versus custom formulation, about ingredients and skin types, about batch freshness, about packaging and labeling, and about samples before they commit. They find manufacturers through search guides, supplier directories, trade referrals, and recommendations from other professionals in the room. This is the vertical our flagship client works in, and the one our live funnel proves out: see the Renaud Naturals funnel.

Supplements

Buyers here are brand founders, retailers, gyms and wellness shops, and distributors. They ask about formats — capsules, powders, liquids — about flavors and serving sizes, about labeling and what testing is included, and about how a first run is structured. They find manufacturers through search, trade directories, and referrals from others in the wellness trade. LabelOrigin offers supplement marketing as a service area of its own, with its own buyer funnel: see the supplements funnel.

Apparel

Buyers here are brands, merch programs, retailers, and teams and organizations ordering for their own people. They ask about blanks and fabric, about custom dyeing and relabeling, about sizes and decoration, and about how reorders work when a style sells through. They find manufacturers through search, trade shows, and referrals. LabelOrigin offers apparel marketing as a service area of its own, with its own buyer funnel: see the apparel funnel.

What LabelOrigin does not do

Two boundaries keep this service honest. First, we do not manufacture anything. We do not formulate, fill, label, or ship products. The manufacturer makes the product; we bring the buyers. Second, we do not buy attention. Every buyer we bring in arrives through organic search, guides, and outreach — never through bought placement. If a manufacturer wants a funnel, qualification, and routing, that is the work. If it wants someone to make the product, that is the manufacturer's own craft, and we leave it to them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Private label marketing
Which verticals do you work with?

Skincare and personal care, supplements, and apparel — the three areas covered on this page. We also talk to manufacturers in adjacent product categories, such as hemp and botanicals, packaging, and food and beverage, when the buyer is a wholesale brand.

Do you work with custom formulation or only stock formulas?

Both. Some manufacturers offer stock formulas a brand can label as its own, and some develop formulas to a brand's specification. Our flagship client, Renaud Naturals, offers private-label stock formulas and custom formulation, and our funnels ask buyers which route they want so the manufacturer hears it from the start.

How are leads qualified?

Each funnel asks about business type, order volume, and program interest before a lead reaches a sales desk. Inquiries that do not meet the client's minimums don't reach the sales inbox. Not the right fit yet? We'll suggest other options or revisit when the timing's better. Every submission carries a reference ID and the buyer's own answers.
